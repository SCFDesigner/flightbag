DME Arc Trainer

Quick Reference

On the arc: heading ≈ radial ± 90° — clockwise +90, counterclockwise −90. Keep the bearing pointer on the wingtip.

10-10 rule: when the pointer falls ~10° behind the wingtip, turn 10° toward the station. Outside the arc, turn in more; inside, turn out.

Joining: lead the 90° turn by about your turn radius — GS ÷ 200 NM (½ NM at 100 kts).

Leaving: lead radial ≈ 60 × lead ÷ DME degrees before the final course radial (charts often publish an LR).
