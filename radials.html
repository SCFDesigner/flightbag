Degrees between two radials

Subtract the smaller from the larger. If you get more than 180, you went the long way round: take it from 360.

  • 180 → 270: 270 − 180 = 90
  • 350 → 020: 350 − 20 = 330, over 180 → 360 − 330 = 30. Or go through north: 10 to 360, plus 20 = 30.
  • 090 → 270: 180 either way (it's the reciprocal).

Which way to turn

Numbers increase clockwise. Target bigger than current → right turn, unless the gap is over 180 — then the short way is left through north. Exactly 180 is a toss-up.

  • 180 → 270: bigger, 90 apart → right 90
  • 030 → 300: bigger, but 270 apart → left 90 through north
  • 300 → 030: smaller, but 270 apart → right 90 through north

Reciprocal

Add or subtract 180. Quick trick: +200 then −20 for anything under 180, −200 then +20 for anything 180 and up.

  • 040: 040 + 200 = 240, − 20 = 220
  • 310: 310 − 200 = 110, + 20 = 130

On the R-180 your bearing TO the station is the reciprocal, 360. Radial = FROM, bearing = TO.

Timed turns

Standard rate is 3° per second, so seconds = degrees ÷ 3. Half-standard rate doubles it.
