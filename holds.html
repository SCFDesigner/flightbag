Hold Trainer

Quick Reference

Standard hold: right turns, 1-minute inbound legs at or below 14,000 MSL (1½ min above).

Entries (AIM 5-3-8): sectors set by a line 70° to the inbound course — direct 180°, teardrop 70°, parallel 110°.

Timing: start outbound timing abeam the fix (or wings-level). Adjust the outbound leg to make the inbound leg 1 minute.

Wind: correct on the inbound leg, then triple that drift correction on the outbound leg (opposite side).
