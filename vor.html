VOR Trainer

Quick Reference

Radials go from the station. Inbound on the 240 radial = OBS 060 TO; outbound = 240 FROM.

Where am I? Twist the OBS until the needle centres with a FROM flag — that's your radial.

Intercept: set the course, turn 30–45° toward the needle, and start the turn onto course as it comes off full scale.

Wind — bracket: drifting off? Turn 20° toward the needle; once centred, take half out (10°). Keep halving until it stays — that's your wind correction.
